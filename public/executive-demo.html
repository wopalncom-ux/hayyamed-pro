The Challenge

The Current Compliance Gap

Healthcare professionals, employers, and authorities face a fragmented, manual, and disconnected compliance landscape.

Fragmented CME Records

Practitioners track CME manually across certificates, emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, and multiple training sources — scattered and unverified.

Limited Real-Time Visibility

Employers often don't know which professionals are compliant, near renewal deadline, or missing required credits until it's too late.

Manual Reporting Burden

HR, medical education, and compliance teams spend hours collecting documents and preparing reports that should be generated in seconds.

Delayed Renewal Preparation

Many practitioners discover missing CME requirements only weeks before license renewal deadlines — causing avoidable stress and risk.

Training Provider Disconnect

Training centers deliver CME activities without a unified post-training compliance tracking connection to practitioners' wallets.

Government Data Gap

Authorities may lack a live, structured, centralized view of professional development readiness across the full medical workforce.
