Compliance Risks — Prioritized
🟠 HIGH — DPA Template Missing for Employer Clients
Every employer accessing staff compliance data must sign a Data Processing Agreement. This is a legal requirement under Qatar PDPL and GDPR. No DPA template found in the platform. Hospitals in Qatar and UAE will require this before signing any contract. Blocking enterprise sales. Remedy: Draft DPA template with legal counsel. Effort: 2-3 days (legal). Impact: Unblocks enterprise sales.
🟠 HIGH — DPA with Anthropic Not Executed
When processing healthcare professional data through Claude AI, an executed DPA with Anthropic is required under GDPR Article 28 and equivalent PDPL requirements. Standard Anthropic terms may not cover healthcare data processing for EU/GCC patients. Remedy: Execute Anthropic Data Processing Agreement for enterprise customers. Effort: 1 day.
🟠 HIGH — Compliance Rule Accuracy Not Independently Verified
The rules engine contains compliance rules for 8 authorities. However, giving incorrect compliance advice to a healthcare professional could result in a license lapse — a significant legal liability. The platform's CLAUDE.md acknowledges "wrong compliance advice is a liability." Rules must be independently reviewed by licensed healthcare regulatory consultants for each country. Remedy: Engage QCHP-registered consultant to review Qatar rules. Budget: $2,000-5,000 per country review.
🟡 MEDIUM — Explicit AI Consent Checkbox Missing
Healthcare professionals' career data is processed by Claude AI for gap analysis, learning pathways, and renewal predictions. GDPR and PDPL require explicit consent for AI processing of professional data. Privacy settings page exists but explicit "I consent to AI analysis of my professional data" checkbox with plain-language explanation is not clearly evidenced. Remedy: Add explicit AI consent checkbox in onboarding Step 6 (privacy settings). Effort: 4 hours.
🟡 MEDIUM — Disclaimer Missing from Landing Page Hero
The mandatory disclaimer is on the dashboard footer and onboarding but not prominently on the public landing page or marketing pages. Healthcare regulators in GCC may require disclaimers visible to users BEFORE signup. Remedy: Add single-line disclaimer to landing page footer or about section. Effort: 30 minutes.
🟡 MEDIUM — Privacy Policy / ToS Not Yet Reviewed by Licensed Counsel
Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages exist but have not been confirmed to have legal review. Healthcare SaaS involving professional data in Qatar requires PDPL-compliant privacy policies. GCC hospital procurement teams will require legal review evidence. Remedy: Engage licensed Qatar attorney for PDPL review. Budget: $1,500-3,000.
🔵 LOW — No Specialty-Specific CME Category Caps Verified
SCFHS and other authorities have category caps (e.g., max 50% credits from conferences). The compliance_activity_categories table stores these caps. Verify that the rules engine correctly enforces category caps per authority, not just total credit counts. Effort: 2 hours (audit) + possible rules update.
