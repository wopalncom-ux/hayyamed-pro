Product-Market Fit Assessment

Market Signals

✅ Real regulatory requirement: 100,000+ GCC healthcare professionals legally required to track CME annually
✅ Pain is acute: License lapses can end careers — urgency is built in
✅ No dominant competitor: Excel spreadsheets and paper are the current "competition" in Qatar
✅ Multiple monetization paths: Individual, Employer, University, Government, Training Provider
✅ Network effects: More employers → more professionals forced to use it → more employers
⚠️ Sales cycle risk: Hospital IT procurement is 6-18 months — need revenue from individuals first
⚠️ Rule accuracy risk: Single wrong rule = professional liability exposure

PMF Score: 82/100

Strong product-market fit for the GCC healthcare CME market. The regulatory mandate creates guaranteed demand. The platform's breadth (8 authorities, AI, employer compliance, training marketplace) creates a defensible moat.
Key Insight: "Aha moment" = first verified CME activity logged. Once a professional submits their first activity and sees their compliance ring update, the value is immediately clear. Time-to-aha must be <5 minutes from signup.
